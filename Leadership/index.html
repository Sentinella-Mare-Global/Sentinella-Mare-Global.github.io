Our Leadership

James Davies
Founder & CEO

Founder and CEO of Sentinella Mare. James leads the company's strategic direction, the development of its intelligence framework, and its institutional partnerships across the Mediterranean.

He brings more than a decade of federal law enforcement and hybrid threat intelligence experience to the role, with deep coverage of both Latin America and Russia and fluency in Spanish and Russian. His operational background spans all-source intelligence collection, virtual operations, protective intelligence, and disinformation campaigns. That cross-domain experience is the foundation of the MHTES framework that powers Sentinella Mare — a single, operationally validated exposure score that synthesises five threat vectors into one continuously updated picture.

James founded Sentinella Mare to bring the integrated, all-source craft developed inside federal service to the commercial maritime operators who increasingly need it.

Erika Ritzmann
Co-Founder & COO

Co-Founder and COO of Sentinella Mare. Erika leads the company's operational infrastructure, regulatory and compliance posture, and institutional partnership development, translating Sentinella's technical capabilities into deployable solutions for clients and partners.

She brings more than eight years of public policy and management consulting experience to the role, supporting U.S. federal agencies — including the IRS, FEMA, and DHS — on regulatory compliance, emergency management, and data-driven operational improvement. Her background spans national-level emergency management policy and doctrine, business continuity and resilience planning, program evaluation for federally mandated initiatives, and workforce training design grounded in adult learning methodologies.

That cross-functional experience is the foundation of Sentinella Mare's operating architecture — the compliance frameworks, service agreements, and partnership structures that move the MHTES methodology from concept to contract with Mediterranean port authorities, fleet operators, and subsea cable stakeholders.

Erika co-founded Sentinella Mare to give the MHTES framework the institutional backbone it needs to operate credibly with regulators and partners across the Mediterranean.

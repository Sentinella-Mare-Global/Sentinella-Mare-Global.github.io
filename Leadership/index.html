Our Leadership

James Davies
Founder & CEO

Founder and CEO of Sentinella Global. James leads the company's strategic direction, the development of its intelligence framework, and its institutional partnerships across the Mediterranean.

He brings more than a decade of federal law enforcement and hybrid threat intelligence experience to the role, with deep coverage of both Latin America and Russia and fluency in Spanish and Russian. His operational background spans all-source intelligence collection, virtual operations, protective intelligence, and disinformation campaigns. That cross-domain experience is the foundation of the MHTES framework that powers Sentinella Mare — a single, operationally validated exposure score that synthesises five threat vectors into one continuously updated picture.

James founded Sentinella Global to bring the integrated, all-source craft developed inside federal service to the commercial maritime operators who increasingly need it.

Mediterranean maritime intelligence. AI-driven risk intelligence for shipping operators, logistics firms, and institutional investors navigating the world's most strategically complex sea.
